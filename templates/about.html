About Vayum

Vayum brings air quality for Indian cities into one clear page: what you are breathing now, what the last week looked like, and what the next day is likely to bring.

Why it exists

Air quality numbers are published widely, but they are scattered across dashboards that assume you already know what a PM2.5 reading means. Vayum takes the six pollutants that matter, combines them into one index, and says plainly whether it is a good day to be outside.

How a reading is built

  1. Collect — pollutant readings for the selected city are pulled from a monitoring source.
  2. Clean — missing and out-of-range values are handled with pandas before anything is shown.
  3. Combine — each pollutant is scaled against its safe limit; the worst sub-index becomes the overall AQI, as CPCB does it.
  4. Forecast — past readings and weather are used to train a model that projects the next 24 hours.

Where the project stands

The interface, the location data and the Flask structure are complete. The prediction model is not trained yet, so the 24-hour chart shows placeholder values and is labelled as such. Nothing on this site should be used for health decisions.

Built with

Python, Flask, Jinja2, HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with Chart.js for the graphs. Pandas, NumPy and scikit-learn come in at the data stage.
